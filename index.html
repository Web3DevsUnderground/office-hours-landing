FAQ

Who is this for?

Developers, researchers, founders and students with a real question worth twenty minutes of an expert's time. If you're deciding between MPC approaches, stuck on a proof, weighing a research direction, or building something hard — this is for you.

How are slots assigned?

By the hosts themselves. Each host reviews the applications to their own sessions — with our help — and picks the questions they most want to spend twenty minutes on. Speed of applying matters less than the quality of your question. Everyone gets an answer either way.

Can I apply to more than one host?

Yes, up to two. Tell each host what you'd ask them specifically — one pasted generic question to both hurts your odds twice.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The community runs it — free, with love, zero shilling. Powered by Masterkey VC.

What should I prepare?

One question you actually care about, and enough context to reach it fast. Twenty minutes is a long time when the first fifteen aren't spent explaining background.

Where exactly is it?

Startup Nation Central, Lilienblum 28, Tel Aviv — our home venue. Room and arrival instructions come with your confirmation email.

Can I come just for the evening?

Yes — the panel and rooftop cocktails have their own RSVP (80 seats, approval on request). People with a confirmed 1:1 that day are approved first; the rest by fit. RSVP here →

Anything about the date I should know?

Israel's clocks go back one hour on the night of 24→25 October. Your confirmation shows Israel time as it will be on the day — check your arrival time that morning.
